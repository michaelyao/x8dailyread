### Quoting Thariq Shihipar on Prompt Caching
**Link:** https://simonwillison.net/2026/Feb/20/thariq-shihipar/

A brief but important technical insight from Thariq Shihipar at Anthropic about how Claude Code manages costs. Long-running agentic products are made feasible by prompt caching, which allows reuse of computation from previous roundtrips. Claude Code runs alerts on prompt cache hit rates and declares SEVs (severe incidents) if they're too low, as this directly impacts both costs and rate limits for subscription plans.

**Key Insight:** Prompt caching is the critical infrastructure that makes AI coding agents economically viable at scale. High cache hit rates aren't just a performance optimization—they're essential for the business model.
