### Whale Fall
**Link:** https://nesbitt.io/2026/02/21/whale-fall.html

**Summary:** Uses the marine biology concept of "whale fall" (where a dead whale sustains deep-sea ecosystems for decades) as an analogy for unmaintained open source projects. Projects go through stages: mobile scavengers (competing forks like LibreOffice from OpenOffice), enrichment opportunists (tools targeting the original's formats), and chemosynthetic bacteria (protocols/specs that outlast their creators like OCI container specs).
